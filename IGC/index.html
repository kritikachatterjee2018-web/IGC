About This Project

Environmental sustainability through data-driven solutions

Our Mission

This hackathon project aims to combat air pollution in Maharashtra through strategic tree plantation. By analyzing pollution data across 26 cities, we provide actionable insights for environmental restoration.

Impact

Our calculator helps urban planners, environmentalists, and policymakers make informed decisions about tree plantation, potentially reducing pollution levels by up to 30% in targeted areas.
